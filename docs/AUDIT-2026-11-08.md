## Deferred items and coverage limits

- **V-07**, `src/lib/queue.ts:92`: exact filename matching can miss case/Unicode
  aliases after native replacement confirmation. The backend rejects rather
  than overwrites. Naive case folding could confuse distinct files on a
  case-sensitive volume; a future fix should tie consent to backend identity.
- **R2-N2**, `src-tauri/src/jobs/manager.rs:99`: append performs filesystem identity
  checks under the queue mutex. Slow/network volumes could delay queue controls.
  Moving them out needs snapshot/revalidation without losing atomic source safety.
- **R2-N3**, `src-tauri/src/ffmpeg/progress.rs:47`: single-interval ETA can fluctuate;
  short smoothing is a follow-up UX improvement.

Real Dolby Vision was not exercised: synthetic generation lacks the required RPU.
The matrix's zero skipped encodes does not mean Dolby Vision was tested. Real
VoiceOver speech/rotor, OS accessibility preferences, physical Finder drag/drop,
accepting pending restoration, every diagnostic dialog, low-space/removable-volume
faults, crash timing during remux, and all codec/track combinations in the GUI
remain unverified. Automated tests cover several of these underlying paths.
No signed distribution, notarization, release publication, updater installation,
Windows, Linux desktop or Intel Mac validation was performed. Update/release
logic was tested without publishing or installing anything.
